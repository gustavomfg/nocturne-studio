# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md)

## Our Commitment

Nocturne Studio aims to provide a welcoming, respectful and collaborative environment for everyone.

We value constructive technical discussions, mutual respect and open collaboration.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Focus discussions on technical topics and project improvement.
- Be open to constructive feedback.
- Respect different perspectives and experience levels.
- Help maintain a friendly and inclusive environment.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or offensive language.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or development.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Spam or intentionally misleading contributions.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for ensuring this Code of Conduct is respected.

They may edit or remove inappropriate content, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

Public discussions should remain focused on the technical aspects of the project whenever possible.

---

Thank you for helping build a respectful and collaborative community around Nocturne Studio.
